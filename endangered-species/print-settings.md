# Print Settings (Bambu H2C)

## Material Options
- Recommended: PETG or ASA
- Avoid: PLA (heat/UV risk)

## Slicer Settings
- Layer Height: 0.16 (final) / 0.20 (draft)
- Walls: 4
- Top Layers: 6
- Infill: 20% (gyroid)

## Adhesion
- Brim: 8 lines

## Multi-Color Setup
- Frame: Black
- Text: White or light color

### Bambu Studio Workflow
- Import bodies separately or as assembly
- Assign AMS slots per body
- Enable flush into infill
- Reduce flush multiplier to ~0.6–0.7

## Tips
- Enable ironing for smooth finish
- Consider color swap or AMS usage for clean lettering
- Watch for warping on large flat prints
